Returns & Refunds

We want you to love your WAZN sunglasses. If they are not right for you, you may return them within 14 days of delivery.

Conditions

  • Items must be unworn, unscratched, and in their original packaging with all accessories included.
  • Proof of purchase (order number or receipt) is required.
  • For hygiene and safety reasons, we cannot accept returns on items showing signs of wear or damage caused after delivery.

How to start a return

Email us at mind.balance77@gmail.com with your order number and the reason for the return. We will reply with return instructions within 2 business days.

Return shipping

  • If the item arrived damaged, defective, or incorrect, WAZN covers the return shipping cost.
  • For change-of-mind returns, the return shipping cost is the customer's responsibility.

Refunds

Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the outcome. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 14 days. Please note that your bank or card issuer may take additional time to post the refund.

Exchanges

To exchange for a different colour, return the original item and place a new order. This is the fastest way to get the pair you want.

Damaged or wrong items

Please inspect your order on delivery. Contact us immediately if the item is defective, damaged, or not what you ordered, and we will make it right.
